第6步: download_file(url="截图2链接", save_path="downloads/images/screenshots/游戏名_2.jpg")

## 参数说明
- title: 游戏名称
- intro: <p>150字左右的HTML格式简介</p>
- download_url: 游戏APK/文件下载地址
- logo_path: "downloads/images/logo/游戏名_logo.jpg"
- screenshot_paths: '["downloads/images/screenshots/游戏名_1.jpg","downloads/images/screenshots/游戏名_2.jpg","downloads/images/screenshots/游戏名_3.jpg"]'
